# Grounded Lexical Cognitive Frame Layer (GLCFL)

GLCFL is the lexical grounding layer.

## Purpose

Ground words, roles, and relations against constrained reality references before judgment.

## Responsibilities

- lexical unit grounding
- relation-role framing
- ambiguity containment with residual preservation
- support for governed claim formation

## Governance Constraints

- Root/pattern/derivative signals are analytical, not standalone evidence.
- Metaphor cannot become literal certificate without explicit governance.
- Residuals must be preserved when grounding remains incomplete.
