# Formal Scientific Validation Baseline Notes

## Strategic order criteria

1. **adversarial_governance_validation**  
   Entry: baseline governance contracts available.  
   Exit: certificate bypass vectors are blocked by governance gate rules.

2. **property_based_invariant_testing**  
   Entry: adversarial checks exist for single attempts.  
   Exit: generated cases preserve canonical final judgments and block illicit certificate issuance.

3. **distributed_governance_simulation**  
   Entry: single-node invariants are stable.  
   Exit: multi-agent simulation shows no silent certificate escalation.

4. **scientific_benchmarking**  
   Entry: simulation and invariants are reproducible.  
   Exit: benchmark baselines cover attack harness and invariant outcomes.

5. **formal_theorem_system**  
   Entry: benchmark baseline stabilized.  
   Exit: theorem scaffolding is present for no-illicit-certification, monotonicity, and residual persistence.

6. **production_scaling**  
   Entry: formal and benchmark requirements are defined.  
   Exit: scaling work preserves governance gates and residual handling.

7. **external_audit_and_publication**  
   Entry: all internal gates verified.  
   Exit: artifacts are externally auditable and publication-ready.
